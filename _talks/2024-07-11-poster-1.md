At the Deep Learning and Reinforcement Learning (DLRL) Summer School 2024 in Toronto, hosted by CIFAR, Amii, Mila and the Vector Institute, I presented my research on multi-objective reinforcement learning for infrastructural maintenance planning in the poster session.

The poster covered [MO-DCMAC](/publication/mo-dcmac-2025) and its application to the fifty-year maintenance planning problem for Amsterdam's historic quay walls.
