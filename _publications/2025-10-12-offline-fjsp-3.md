**Abstract**  
The Job-Shop Scheduling Problem (JSP) and Flexible Job-Shop Scheduling Problem (FJSP) are canonical combinatorial optimization problems with wide-ranging applications in industrial operations. Recent online RL approaches require millions of simulated interactions and suffer from poor sample efficiency. We propose Conservative Discrete Quantile Actor–Critic (CDQAC), a novel offline RL algorithm that learns effective scheduling policies directly from historical data, eliminating online interactions while improving upon suboptimal training data. CDQAC couples a quantile-based critic with a delayed policy update to estimate the return distribution of each machine–operation pair. Extensive experiments show that CDQAC outperforms the data-generating heuristics and state-of-the-art offline and online RL baselines, and is highly sample efficient—needing only 10–20 training instances. Surprisingly, CDQAC performs best when trained on data generated by a random heuristic, outperforming training on higher-quality GA and PDR data.
